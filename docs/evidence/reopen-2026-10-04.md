What this shows:

- **Zero rounds on any of the ten feeds between Friday 20:00 ET and Sunday 20:00 ET.** Both live credit venues on Base
  priced collateral at the held value for the whole 48-hour closed window, and liquidation stayed permissionless the whole
  time.
- **The first print arrives 23 to 53 seconds after the boundary**, so the first post-reopen block prices a position at a
  value nobody could act on before: the Morpho and Aave liquidation checks move in that block.
- **The gaps this weekend were benign** (−7.5 to +65 bps for nine feeds, +282 bps for MSTRc); the mechanism is the same
  on a bad weekend, only the sign and size change.
