- **Cross-check: 0 mismatches.** For every feed, the reopen run's held answer and `updatedAt`, first round id, answer and
  `updatedAt`, lag and gap equal the chain's, and the close run's held print equals the chain's held print. The
  Observatory saw each first print 3 to 57 seconds after it was transmitted (one tick a minute); the capture's `block` is
  the block of the snapshot that saw it, not the print block.
- **The 15 minutes before each transition were not recorded, and the cause is known.** Both runs armed 40 to 44 seconds
  after the transition instead of 15 minutes before it. Replayed offline, the capture's arming logic arms at
  transition − 900 s for both instants, so the job did not run then. The Cloudflare account's D1 free tier was over its
  daily row-read limit, which resets at 00:00 UTC, and both transitions (20:00 ET under US daylight time) fall exactly at
  00:00 UTC: the cron had no database in the last minutes of each UTC day, and the run started on the first tick after
  the reset.
  D1 refused queries with "Your account has exceeded D1's free tier daily row read limit … wait until tomorrow (midnight
  UTC)" on Mon 12 Oct 16:25 UTC; one unindexed positions query accounted for about 4.2M of the 5M daily reads.
- **`completed_at` stayed null** on both runs although each recorded the whole 90-minute window with no missed tick: a
  run is marked complete only by a tick at exactly transition + 5400 s, the second the window closes (a boundary bug,
  fixed separately). The summaries themselves are complete.
