The price stops at 8 p.m. Friday. Liquidation doesn’t.

Coinbase’s tokenized stocks trade on Base around the clock. Their Chainlink price feeds hold Friday’s close until Sunday 8 p.m., and the lending markets for them keep pricing loans on that number, with liquidation open all weekend. Boundline is the credit layer that refuses a frozen price and protects your position at the live one.

What Boundline allows, and when

Boundline’s markets check the clock before they trust the price. While the feed is held, anything that needs a price stops. Anything that only makes a loan safer never does.

What a session-safe Boundline market allows while the feed is live and while it is held
ActionFeed livepre-market to overnightFeed heldweekend, holiday, stale or paused

On Boundline’s session-safe Morpho markets the oracle, SessionGuard, reverts while the feed is held, so Morpho itself refuses the borrow, the withdrawal and the liquidation. Repaying and adding collateral never read the oracle, so they always work.

A verdict per stock, every block

SessionGate reads the NYSE calendar, the feed, Coinbase’s OracleRegistry and the stock token itself. The first check that applies wins, and any failed read fails closed.

  1. MARKET_CLOSEDThe calendar says the market is shut: a weekend or a holiday.
  2. FEED_PAUSEDCoinbase’s OracleRegistry has paused the feed.
  3. TRANSFER_PAUSEDThe issuer has paused transfers of the stock token.
  4. FEED_INVALIDThe feed’s answer cannot be used.
  5. FEED_STALEThe last print is older than the limit.
  6. OPENThe price is safe to use.

Base holds the rescue to the frozen price

Each protective action is sent as a Cobalt validity transaction with a condition on the feed’s storage slot. Base includes it only while the feed still shows the round the keeper decided on, so a rescue planned on a frozen price can never land on a different one.

While the feed is held, protection prices positions with ShadowGuard: the Aerodrome one-hour average, less 2%. Inside a narrow band below the liquidation line, Morpho’s audited pre-liquidation sells a small slice of collateral and repays the debt; elsewhere a repay runs down a funding ladder: the borrower’s reserve, the partner’s, then a bounded advance from the Boundline Reserve.
