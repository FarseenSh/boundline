What a weekend does to a loan

The stock keeps trading on Aerodrome all weekend. The price feed that Aave and Morpho read stops at 8 p.m. Friday and holds that number until 8 p.m. Sunday. Then the whole weekend's move prints at once, and a loan that slipped past its limit is liquidated before its owner can act.

No weekend liquidation has happened on Base yet: the reopen gaps so far were small, from −7.5 to +282 bps. The risk is the exposure. Debt priced on the frozen feed on Saturday grew from $0.51M to $1.56M over the last three weekends, with liquidation open the whole time.

How Boundline handles the weekend

Boundline puts the market clock into the loan. Borrowing and liquidation pause while the feed is frozen; protection keeps running at the price the market actually shows.

  1. 1Fri 20:00 ET

    Refuse

    SessionGuard stops pricing the frozen feed, so on Boundline's markets nobody can borrow, withdraw or liquidate against it. Repay and add-collateral always work.

  2. 2Saturday

    Watch the live price

    ShadowGuard prices every position at the Aerodrome one-hour average less 2%, the price the market actually shows.

  3. 3Saturday, in the band

    Protect

    A small slice of collateral is sold inside Morpho's audited pre-liquidation, or the loan is repaid down the funding ladder: your reserve, the partner's, then a bounded Reserve advance. Base includes the keeper's transaction only while the feed is still frozen.

  4. 4Sun 20:00 ET

    Reopen safely

    The feed prints and the position is already safe. Any advance is settled in the next open session, with its fee.

Borrow in one tap, in your currency

One signature sends one batch. Boundline never holds your stock or your loan: you keep your own position and let the desk manage it, inside the partner's policy and the session check.

Wallets and brokers embed the same flow through the SDK. The Boundline app is partner #1 of its own SDK.

      For partners and agents

      A wallet, broker or neobank offers a credit line against stocks in its own app, under its own policy. Boundline runs the session checks, the protection and the record of every decision.

      Partner API

      Quotes with reason codes: capacity rounds down, charges round up, unknown data fails closed. Obligations, decision timelines, policy versions and HMAC-signed webhooks. Keyless public routes are paid per call over x402, $0.01 each.
