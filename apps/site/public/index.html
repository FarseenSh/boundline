    What Boundline does.

    It refuses a frozen price.

    While the feed holds Friday's close, Boundline's markets stop pricing it: no new borrows, no withdrawals, no liquidations. Repaying and adding collateral never stop.

    SessionGuard, a Morpho oracle that reads an onchain NYSE calendar and a verdict on every price. It fails closed.

    It protects you at the live price.

    The stock keeps trading on Aerodrome all weekend. Boundline prices your loan there, and when it drifts toward its limit, it repays a little or sells a small slice of collateral before the reopen can hurt you.

    ShadowGuard, Morpho's audited pre-liquidation, a funding ladder that ends at the Boundline Reserve, and Cobalt validity transactions that Base includes only while the feed is still frozen.

    It pays you in your currency.

    One tap sends one batch: authorise, add collateral, draw, and swap the USDC into euros on the way out. The euros arrive with the loan.

    On Base Sepolia, 0.5 USDC became 0.46 EURCt inside the borrow flow, behind the same session check: 0x9090633c…5b5bd3.

    For wallets, brokers and agents.

    Offer a credit line against stocks without running the risk layer. The Boundline app is the first partner of its own SDK.

    Partner API
    Quotes with reason codes, obligations, decision timelines and HMAC-signed webhooks. Public routes are metered over x402.
    SDK
    Reads, quotes and every user-signed call, batched into one wallet_sendCalls with the local-money payout.
    Console
    Obligations, line timelines, policy versions, drills with recorded runs, and an evidence export.
    Agents
    An MCP server with session, positions, shadowHealth, quote, protect and route. It returns plans and calldata; the agent's own wallet signs.

    Non-custodial: users keep their own Morpho or Aave position and authorise Boundline with one signature, revocable in one call.

    const calls = boundline.prepare.borrowBatch({
  lineId, draw: 100_000_000n, needsAuthorization: false,
  payout: { token: "EURC", minOut, to: user,
            deadline: payoutDeadline(), gateToken: AAPLc },
});
// authorise, add collateral, draw, swap: one batch
await wallet.sendCalls(boundline.buildSendCalls(calls));
