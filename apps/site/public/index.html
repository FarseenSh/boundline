Borrow against your stocks on Base. Paid in your currency. Never liquidated while Wall Street is closed.

Coinbase tokenized stocks trade on Base around the clock, but their price feeds hold Friday's close from 20:00 ET until Sunday night. The live credit venues read that held price and liquidate at any hour. Boundline is the session-aware credit layer underneath them: an onchain NYSE calendar, oracles that refuse to price a frozen feed, protection that runs seven days a week at the price the market actually shows, self-custody credit lines any wallet or broker can embed, and a public observatory that watches it all.

How it works

Three gates, one funding ladder, one record. Every contract is verified on Base Sepolia today and fork-proven against the live mainnet venues.

SessionGuard

A Morpho oracle that returns a price only while the NYSE calendar says open and the feed is fresh. While Wall Street is closed, borrow, withdrawCollateral and liquidate revert MarketClosed(WEEKEND); repay and supplyCollateral still work.

ShadowGuard + Protector

A pre-liquidation oracle that prices at the Aerodrome 1h TWAP while the feed is held, so a gentle partial pre-liquidation can run on a Saturday at the price the market shows, and pays for itself from the seized collateral.

Validity transactions

The keeper pre-signs its rescues and Base itself includes them only while the feed slot still equals the held round, or in the first block after it prints a new one. Cobalt's base_sendRawTransactionValidity, used for what it was built for.

CreditDesk lines

Partner-scoped, self-custody credit lines on the user's own Morpho position: per-ticker LTV caps under the venue's LLTV, draw bounds, exposure caps, eligibility hooks, and a decision hash on every action.

Aave V4 as the second venue

AaveDesk draws through Aave's Taker, the ProtectionVault repays through the Giver at any hour down a funding ladder (user reserve, partner reserve, bounded advance) and recovers in the next open session.

Protection reserve and the Boundline Reserve

On Morpho desk lines the DeskProtectionVault repays at any hour down the same ladder: the user's own USDC reserve first, then the partner's, then a bounded advance funded by the Boundline Reserve, an ERC-4626 vault with a cooldown and a utilisation cap. The advance is settled later with its fee, which the Reserve folds into its share price.

Local money

The borrow batch ends with a swap of the drawn USDC into EURC or another local stablecoin on the pool the partner configured, slippage-bounded, deadline-bound and session-checked. One tap, your currency.

For partners

Boundline is its own first partner: the app above is the SDK's reference implementation. A wallet, a broker or a neobank embeds the same line in a day.

Partner API

Keys, lines, deterministic quotes with reason codes (capacity rounds down, charges round up, unknown data fails closed), obligations, decision timelines, policy versions, drills, reconciliation and an evidence export. Every response carries a correlation id, the policy version and a quote hash. Public quotes are metered with x402 on Base Sepolia.

SDK and widget

@boundline/sdk (viem only): read a line, quote it, prepare every user-signed call, batch them for wallet_sendCalls with a paymaster and a Builder Code, poll Flashblocks preconfirmations, and end the batch in local money.

const calls = boundline.prepare.borrowBatch({
  lineId, draw: 100_000_000n, needsAuthorization: false,
  payout: { token: "EURC", minOut, to: user,
            deadline: payoutDeadline(), gateToken: AAPLc },
});
await wallet.sendCalls(boundline.buildSendCalls(calls));

Keeper and indexer

A keeper that mirrors the mainnet rounds onto the Sepolia simulators and arms protections as validity transactions; an indexer with hash-chained daily roots and a reconciliation job against the venues.

Agent surface (MCP)

An MCP server over streamable HTTP for agents with wallets: session, positions, shadowHealth, quote, protect and route, over the same live services, with REST aliases. Read-only: it returns plans and calldata, the agent's own wallet signs.
